NEMESIS - the chess opponent that learns how you play

1. Right-click (or Control-click) "Nemesis" and choose "Open", then click "Open" again.
   (A normal double-click is blocked the first time because this free app isn't signed by Apple.
   After the first time, double-clicking works.)
2. A Terminal window opens, and Nemesis opens in your web browser.
   Keep the Terminal window open while you play. Close it to quit.
3. Type your Chess.com or Lichess username, and Nemesis studies your games.

If macOS still refuses: open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down,
and click "Open Anyway" next to Nemesis.

Your games and everything Nemesis learns stay on this computer (in your home folder, under .nemesis).
