NEMESIS - the chess opponent that learns how you play

1. Double-click Nemesis.exe.
2. The first time, Windows may show "Windows protected your PC".
   That's because this free app isn't code-signed. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
3. A small black window opens, and Nemesis opens in your web browser.
   Keep the black window open while you play. Close it to quit.
4. Type your Chess.com or Lichess username, and Nemesis studies your games.

Your games and everything Nemesis learns stay on this computer (in your user folder, under .nemesis).
