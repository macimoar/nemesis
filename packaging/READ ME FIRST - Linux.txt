NEMESIS - the chess opponent that learns how you play

1. Run ./Nemesis (from a terminal, or double-click it if your file manager allows).
   If it won't run, make it executable first: chmod +x Nemesis
2. Nemesis opens in your web browser. Keep the terminal open while you play; press Ctrl+C to quit.
3. Type your Chess.com or Lichess username, and Nemesis studies your games.

Your games and everything Nemesis learns stay on this computer (in ~/.nemesis).
